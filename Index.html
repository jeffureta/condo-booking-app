⚡ Appliance Operating Guides & Troubleshooting

❄️ Split-Type Air Conditioner

Living Area & Bedroom
  1. Use the remote mounted in the wall cradle beside the bedroom door.
  2. Press the POWER button once to turn ON.
  3. Set mode to COOL (snowflake icon).
  4. Recommended comfortable temperature: 22°C to 24°C on AUTO fan speed.
💡 Troubleshooting: Ensure balcony doors and windows are closed completely. Please turn OFF the unit when leaving to conserve energy.

📺 Android Smart TV & Netflix

Living Area
  1. Press the red POWER button on the TV remote.
  2. Ensure the TV input is set to Android Home.
  3. Select Netflix or YouTube on the dashboard.
  4. Use the active guest profile or sign in with your account.
💡 Troubleshooting: If an app becomes unresponsive, hold the remote power button for 5 seconds to perform a quick reboot.

🍳 Induction Cooktop

Kitchen Counter
  1. Place the provided induction-compatible pot/pan on the cooking circle first.
  2. Press the ON/OFF sensor switch.
  3. Select Fry or Boil mode and adjust temperature with +/- buttons.
💡 Troubleshooting: If the cooktop beeps and shows E0, it means no induction pan is detected on the surface. Check the child-lock button (press and hold lock icon for 3s if buttons don't react).

🚿 Instant Shower Water Heater

Bathroom
  1. Lift the main shower water lever to initiate water flow.
  2. Turn the rotary temperature dial clockwise from LOW to MED.
  3. Always verify water is actively running through the showerhead before adjusting heat.
💡 Troubleshooting: If water is not hot enough, slightly reduce water pressure on the lever so the heating chamber can warm the water thoroughly.
